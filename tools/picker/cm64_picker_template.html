CM-64 PCM Picks

For each GM program, choose which half of a CM-64 plays it under Voodoo: the LA half (the MT-TO-GM bank), a PCM part, or both layered. Pick the PCM tone and a level for each half. Changes save as you click.

Connecting…

How Duality uses this. With two or more units, a channel whose program is PCM or Layer takes one of the CM-64s' PCM parts (6 per unit, 31 partials each) and plays the tone named here; LA programs stay on the LA maps. On a single CM-64 the PCM parts sit on channels 9 and 11–15 and always play PCM, so every program's PCM tone matters there even when its half is LA.

Levels scale the file's volume (CC7) on that half: the PCM level applies whenever a PCM part plays the program, the LA level only to a layer. Partials come from the manual's sound list: a 2-partial tone takes twice the voices, so 15 notes fill a CM-64's PCM half. Card sounds (PC 65–128) are not offered yet.
