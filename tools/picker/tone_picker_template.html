How Anima picks today. Per part, the song's seed chooses once per program change: a CM-64 tone about 1 time in 4 when the program has one, otherwise an SC-8850 variation (CC00 ≠ 0) about 2 times in 3, otherwise the capital or an older map's version of it. The file's own bank select always wins; Anima only varies parts the file leaves on the capital.

Weights work like the EFX picker: favoured counts twice, less often counts half, unticked is never picked. EFX runs from much drier (−2) to much wetter (+2) for the insert when this tone has one. One-shot means every sample in the tone plays once and stops (read from the SC-8850 ROM); attack layer means part of it does.
