Choose which SC-8850 insertion effects each instrument family can get. The list follows the SC-8850 manual's numbering; the SC-88Pro has the same 64 types. Changes save as you click.

Connecting…

Families are listed in GM order, like the tone picker; the number on the left is the first GM program of each. P is its priority, the order insert boxes are handed out: a family with a lower P number takes a box from one with a higher number. Duality picks one type per box from the ticked list using the song's seed; each press of Favour adds a step (×2, ×3, ×4 …) and each press of Less often takes one away (×½, ×⅓ …), so the opposite button steps back toward normal; the percentage is that type's share of the family's picks, kept between 1% and 99%. Hex pairs are the MSB/LSB written to 40 03 00. Seat unit is not an instrument family: it is the insert a spare unit gets while it carries harmony, and any family takes that unit when it needs it.
